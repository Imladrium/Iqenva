> # Catenas
> ![[RaemarCatena.png]]
> <small><center><i>The Catena of Ráemar</i></small></center><br>

Catenas act as conduits that alter how the Dark Field interacts with reality, leading to localized regions of space where the laws of physics can be different from the standard. They form the basis for all of the Catenic Arts, and are used across all formerly–[[Felari|Syranui]] space, including Iqenva.

# The Dark Field
Each of the 4 fundamental forces has its own field associated with it—however, each of these is made manifest from a substratum of Reality known as the Dark Field. The 4 forces come from the Dark Field uniformly across spacetime and dictate the nature of the universe.

Catenas break this; they hijack how the Dark Field interacts with Reality in a local area, to a given extent. They act as conduits reaching through both Reality and the Dark Field, augmenting and controlling how things are made manifest.

# The History of the Catenas
The origins of the catenas are shrouded in mystery on Iqenva, but they are known to share connections to the [[Felari|High Felari]]. High Felari often use them to grant select powers for their followers, but there are select catenas that are not seemingly controlled by them. The main example of this are the 11 [[Literamancy|Literamantic Catenas]], which are wholly autonomous from the whims of any High Felari (though created by Qirāsu), and are solely controlled by the [[Reithi]]. 

Historically, almost anything unusual was attributed to a unique catena, but with time it became evident that some strange phenomena are merely resultant from a few common catenas, most of which are either related to Literamancy, Aquamancy, or Isomancy.

Some religions venerate catenas.

# Characteristics of Catenas
Catenas have many forms and descriptions, but all share at least 2 common traits:

- They exhibit a sphere of influence about themselves, with the strength of the effect dependent upon the square of the distance
- They are autonomous, and do not need any form of input energy to operate

Most catenas are made of the same (seemingly) unbreakable substance – a glassy, black stone–like material. The shape of each can vary in accordance with its purpose, and similar catenas often exhibit identifying characteristics with one another.

Catenas usually have inscriptions on the side of them, in a script resembling the known writing of the High Felari. Every catena has a mechanism by which it can impart a given amount of prerequisite knowledge as to its purpose, though the translations for non–Felari can be obfuscated and unhelpful. As such, a great deal of study has gone into the translations of High Felari, some of which has been deliberately hindered and further obfuscated.

Catenas can be used as gateways between Reality and the Dark Field substratum, but knowledge of this is shrouded in myth and purposefully obscured by some Felari, both Low and High. A few Felari make the Dark Field via some catenas their homes; Reality bleeds through a catena into the Dark Field the same way the Dark Field bleeds into Reality, allowing for habitation in the Dark Field near more powerful catenas.

Catenas can only modulate so much of the Dark Field, and their "output" is capped. Effectively, this means there is a limit to the amount a catena can be used; the limits of catenas are unevenly weighted, with some having higher limits than others. In places with a limited amount of magic, the rights to it is often bought and sold the same way glyphs are. Usually, the cost of using a portion of catenic energy is a tax put onto the sale and production of glyphs, though some societies have taken more liberal (and draconian) implementations of catenic regulation.

# Dangers of Catenas
Catenas can be very powerful objects, but also made to be extremely dangerous, often through sheer ignorance. Iqenvans do not know how to make catenas, and their secrets have been lost even among the Low Felari. Some High Felari are aware of how to make them, but there is a memorandum of understanding that such creations are not in the safe interests of the planet. Qirāsu was admonished for Literamancy, Hyanéq for Aquamancy, and Qiriýtaq for Isomancy... so there is a precedent for this pact being broken.

Even so, catenas have had far–reaching and unintentional implications. Iqenvan life has reacted curiously to the presence of the more powerful catenas (Literamancy, Aquamancy, and Isomancy). Certain lifeforms have taken up the ability to refine the energy from catenas and use it as a food source, often microbial life that mutualistically inhabits a specific plant alike to how algae may inhabit a coral. These microbes often supplement the plant's growth, allowing them to grow far faster than other comparable plants.

The consumption of "catenic plants" or anything grown in the vicinity of a powerful catena can transfer these microbes, a rare few of which were able to adapt to a new ecosystem inside the Reithi. While these microbes are plentiful around catenas, they are nearly non–existent in their mutated forms elsewhere. People who inhabit the region close to catenas often become hosts, and these microbes symbiotically help provide energy to their hosts in exchange for protection. This means people living near powerful catenas generally are more powerful, but also will suffer withdrawal if they move too far abroad for too long a time as the microbes die inside of them. In severe cases, this can result in death.

This phenomenon, known in most of [[Nulâašilu]] as being "Islebound" after the inhabitants of [[Dānura]] who are often affected and bound to the island & surrounding area by it, has resulted in decrees such as the Statute of Aniŧēras, prohibiting immigration and long-term visitation of Dānura to most people in Ancu society, and the formation of a distinct Dānuran identity. The catena of [[Sastranivê]] resulted in the migration of Dānurans to the [[Sastranivê|Eastern Archipelago]], as a safe place for them to live—away from the constricting condominium status of modern Dānura—with their condition.
