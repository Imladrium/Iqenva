‍The Cētu system, also known as Cetuāŋa, is the planetary system about the K–class star Cētu.

# Etymology
The [[Iŋatra|Iŋatre]] name "Cētu" comes from the [[Ancurāna|Ancu]] word "Xētye" which was a personal name, meaning "Brilliant" or "Lustrous". Thereby, "Cetuāŋa" means "Cētu's Domain" (with Iŋatre "-āŋa" being a possessive for a physical area) which at the time included all of the heavens; the meaning has subsequently shrunk to include only the Cētu system.

# Planets
Cētu is approximately 5 billion years⊕ old, with the planets forming shortly after that. In list of distance from Cētu, the 6 planets are: [[Iqenva]], [[Ţavúna]], Letula, Yîluûr, Miŧi, and Cāžaliŷ, the first three rocky and the last three gaseous.

Iqenva is a temperate, tidally–locked world with the only indigenous population of sapient species in Iqenva. The planet started out in a resonance with Cētu, but the effect of its moons and the proximity to its star has over time slowed its rotation.

Ţavúna is also habitable, albeit significantly colder than Iqenva. It is currently in a warmer state of climate, even so it is engulfed in polar ice down to the mid–latitudes (aided by shading by Ţavúna's extensive rocky ring system), with a cool–to–temperate equatorial region. Telescopes on Iqenva are sufficient enough to glean this information, and there is much speculation as to the habitability of the planet.

Letula is a dry world with a thin atmosphere, frigid temperatures, and damaging cosmic rays. It has substantial metallic deposits, but few other redeeming qualities.

Between Letula and Yîluûr lies the frost line, and the asteroid belt. It has 4 known dwarf–planets / notable asteroids.

Yîluûr is the largest planet, with a thick hydrogen / helium atmosphere and a substantial moon system. The three largest moons are outside of the radiation belts of the planet, but far inside the magnetosphere, shielding these worlds from substantial radiation.

Miŧi is an ice giant with a significant ring system, and the smallest of the gaseous planets. It remained as the outermost known planet for several millennia, being on the cusp of visibility in deep Starside regions.

Cāžaliŷ is the outermost known planet since the advent of modern astronomy on Iqenva, taking a sweeping elliptical orbit that renders it very difficult to see at its apogee. It crosses paths with a few dwarf planets not yet discovered on Iqenva.
