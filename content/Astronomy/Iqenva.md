Iqenva is the 1st planet from the K–class star [[Cētu]], and the only astronomical body in the system known to be inhabited by life. The planet is tidally locked to Cētu due to its proximity; though with an unusually high eccentricity and an axial obliquity improbable for such a tidally–locked state, the boundaries of day and night shift over the course of a year. Colloquially, these regions of permanent day and night are known as the '[[Sunside]]' and '[[Starside]]', with the region of temporary day and night colloquially known as the '[[Sunside#Duskring|Duskring]]'. This usage is less standard, and the average location of the terminator line between night and day still denotes the boundary between the Sunside and Starside along the ±90° meridians.
Most of the planet is ocean, with the majority of the oceans located on the Starside, frozen under permanent sea ice. Iqenva has plate tectonics akin to several other habitable planets, resulting in a series of continents; the continents are currently in a state of breakup from the last [supercontinent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercontinent) formation, but still markedly adjacent to one another, clustered relatively nearby in the same hemisphere. This arrangement has helped for intercontinental migrations among the local populations before the advent of recent aeronautical technologies.

# Etymology
In [[Ancurāna|Ancu]] and its daughter–languages, the name for the planet "Iqenva" developed from the proto–Ancu word _Êxunve_; originally meaning "'hallowed' (or 'enshrined') trial". The usage of the translation 'trial' suggests that the ancient Ancu believed in the world being a path towards a higher plane of existence after death; these ideas evolved into the modern [[Sâiýme]] denominations. From "Iqenva" came the name "Iqu" which literally translates to "Hallowed" in [[Caduča|Cadêčí]].

# Formation & Natural History
Iqenva formed shortly after Cētu did, 5.07 Gya⊕ (5.07 billion Earth–years ago). Akin to Earth and many other habitable planets, Iqenva underwent a period of heavy bombardment early in its formation, before cooling. Iqenva has two moons: Zêya and Vêŧa, orbiting in 2:1 [orbital resonance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_resonance) with one another. The moons and the proximity of Iqenva to Cētu tidally locked the planet over time, but this happened with an orbital eccentricity and axial obliquity remaining to give Iqenva its [distinct day–night patterns](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjHWHS6UJg_UgC0BMEYVykiPO8to6gAzut0Ztf631fR7hqSDIVUESsGdQwU6KtqRlTI2KqRnRehoAKaiaUoVsyW0ibbcczMnkdsTdaC3q-geGrnUe-KIUjQF__kTFEAmduajoICLhE7qaC-/s1444/clock+world.png).

Iqenva experiences plate tectonics, just like Earth. The only difference is that, due to the gradual effect of tidal locking, supercontinents had a tendency to form either at the substellar point, or the antistellar point. During these cycles, the climate of the planet would change substantially, because an ocean at the [substellar point](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2020/12/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-ivd.html#mechanismsoftidallockedclimates) is needed for global heat distribution. This has led to multiple extinction events in the past.

# Present–Day Continents
In the present, the majority of land is on the [[Sunside]], and the majority of oceans (frozen over) on the [[Starside]]. There are 3 main continents, and a greater number of regions.

The largest continent is [[Ŋašilu]], west of the [[Sunside|Sunpole]]. It is often divided into two subcontinents ([[Nulâašilu]] in the south, [[Cresanu]] in the north) with a sometimes–omitted third subcontinent in the west ([[Cresanu#Merŋulêam|Merŋulêam]]) added to Cresanu. Nulâašilu is one of the most technologically developed regions of Iqenva, but also has had more wars than is usual across the rest of the planet. Much of Cresanu is nomadic, but there are sedentary nations too that have consolidated the majority of the population. 

North of Ŋašilu is [[Anselesna]], the smallest of the three main continents but also the most mysterious. Certain political and supernatural forces have shut Anselesna and the people there off from the rest of the world, partially by their own desires. Anselesna tapers off into [[Anselesna#Lanuvša|Lanuvša]], which is a ribbon of mountainous land that encircles the north pole. 

[[Istarthu]] is the last major continent, and features a far more diverse range of climates than even Ŋašilu. Much of the continent has seen a long period of stability, but this has come at the cost of technological development, and life there has remained very traditionally–pastoral in several regions. 

South of Istarthu lies the archipelago of [[Sastranivê]], which one of the warmest and wettest regions of Iqenva. Much of these islands are covered in thick rainforests, but the volcanic highlands have seen substantial development at the hands of settlers from Istarthu, and more–so Nulâašilu. 

Lastly, west of Merŋulêam on the Starside, is [[Starside#Lanicas|Lanicas]]. Often known as the "Dragon's Tail" due to its treacherous land and sinuous shape, Lanicas is the coldest region of the planet, with plentiful volcanoes and surrounded by dark, frozen oceans. A unique biosphere revolving around geothermal heat has developed here, unlike anything else on the planet; from this a small population of Reithi have eked out a living here.
