‍The Felari are the least populous of the 3 sentient species of [[Iqenva]], and unrelated to both the [[Reithi]] and the [[Velsari]]. Extremely reclusive, Felari societies tend to eschew trade and diplomacy with the Reithi and Velsari, instead often acting defensively and refusing admittance to their lands to any but themselves. Their technological status is unknown, and their ways of life closely guarded.

There are two main groupings: Low Felari and High Felari. Low Felari tend to live on the periphery of the world near the terminator line in the mountains, though there are some cultures that live further towards the sunside. The High Felari are extremely rare, barely in the dozens of individuals, and are known to have direct ties to the [[catenas]] of Iqenva—and several religions.

# Etymology
The exonym "Felari" comes from [[Ancurāna|Ancu]] "Fēluar" which means "Secretive" or "Reclusive", plus the suffix "-i" indicating agency in a similar manner to English's "-er" suffix. Hence, "Felari" means "The Reclusive Ones."

# Origin
Little is known about the Low Felari's past, but they seem to dwell mostly in the remotest areas of the planet furthest from Reithi settlements, with a few small exceptions in [[Sastranivê]]. This includes Merŋulêam in western [[Cresanu]], the Háan Mountains nearby, and eastern [[Istarthu]]. They are few in number, but cover wide expanses of land for themselves. They do not share many of the lusts for power or worship that the High Felari are often suseptible to, and High Felari scorn their 'Low' brethren.

The High Felari are far more mysterious: they hold direct sway over much of the planet, and have founded multiple religions. Where they come from and why they chose Iqenva is unknown. In the [[War of Wings]], multiple High Felari used Reithi nations for the purposes of indirect fighting and conquest, which ultimately led to the [[Anselesna|Sealing of Anselesna]].

# Appearance
They are most analogous to certain types of pterosaurs in Earth's past. Usually from head to tail, they are 6'–11' long, averaging around 8'. They have a proportional wingspan; 20’ to 35’. For their size they are relatively light. Males and females are roughly equivalent in size. Their patterns and hues vary in accordance with genetics and location; while patterns are contiguous across the entirety of their skin and can be any distinct hues uniformly, the solid colours can vary. On their backside, they often feature either purple, dark green, or neutral colours. On their underside, they usually have some shade of pale blue or white, as an evolutionary development for camouflage against the sky when flying - however black can occur too: patterns are also dependent on genetics.

Felari have exceptionally good eyesight in comparison to many other species, they can also see some ultraviolet wavelengths. Their eyes are often green, blue, yellow, grey, or any hue in-between; they have 4 of them (two smaller eyes next to the regular ones) and they can be controlled individually. They have highly dexterous claws, with 6 talons.

Their skin is leathery, which is curious given their inhabitance of some of the colder regions of the planet. They also have no feathers.

# Low Felari Habits
Low Felari live both in semi–nomadic and sedentary patterns, sometimes overlapping with one another. They never war with one another, but only the Reithi should they attempt to settle or lay claim to any of the land the Felari demarked unto themselves. ‍The Felari are omnivorous, but show a strong preference for eating meat. Herds of livestock are often allowed free reign but are monitored, before being culled. ‍

Some among the Reithi have attempted to determine the magical extent of the Low Felari and see how much they relate to their High Felari brethren, often to the point to foolishness or death: it is highly likely the Low Felari are in possession of one or more [[Catenas|Catenas]], used only by themselves and not shared with the rest of Iqenva. The documentation of any new potential catenas is the primary catalyst for the intrusion into Low Felari affairs by the Reithi, especially by the country of [[Esaceš]]. ‍ Esaceš nominally claims some of the land they inhabit in the Háan region, but after several conflicts with the Low Felari present they have withdrawn settlers from the area.

Felari (both High and Low) do not tend to use or wear clothing, with the exception of jewellery and other ornamentations. ‍

# High Felari Habits
High Felari are known to war with one another, but always via indirect means and using proxies. Several dispute the amount they should control the Reithi and Velsari, some offer their blessings to those who oppose the more militant High Felari. They are known to use catenas to benefit select groups and peoples. The reasons they do not directly war with one another is presumed to be related to their power: a fear of mutual destruction, and even a wish among the most militant High Felari to not see Iqenva laid to waste. The few fragments of High Felari writing known by the Reithi are related to the scripts present on Catenas, also showing strong resemblances to unfurled glyphs.

> [!Origin of the Felari|c-green]-
> ## Origin
> Felari, endonymically known as the Syranui, come from a world known as Yruil. Over time, this relatively standard world would come to experiment upon the Dark Field, leading to the first Catenas. These experimental Catenas decimated research space stations and sometimes entire moons, and public outrage over the continued experimentation upin the Dark Field led to a revolution. The Syranui Union splintered between the capital of Yruil and one of the other planets in the system, Helar. 
> 
> Helar would continue to experiment upon the Dark Field, much to the chagrin of the Yruilans. With perseverance however, the first commercially and militarily useful Catenas came into existence, such as ones that could locally alter gravity enough to allow for the creation of exotic matter, and wormholes. Wormholes, known as hyperlanes, were set up by Helaran authorities to access nearby systems. Yruil looked on with worry and envy, as Helar looked outwards to the stars. Diplomatic concessions over access to the hyperlanes involved Yruil reuniting with Helar, reforming the Syranui Union, this time with the capital on a space station orbiting Helar (Ebithrycu Orbital).
> 
> Colonization and expansion of the Syranui Union went on for hundreds of years, unabatedly. Life was sparse in their region of their galaxy, but now and then worlds with habitable conditions and emergent life were found. Different chirality and biochemistry meant that Syranui could not easily adapt to these worlds: however, it eventually became a nascent technology to genetically engineer people and progenitors for populations to suit these different biospheres. Other Syranui would have to sequester themselves from the local biosphere, eating food grown in biodomes and on space stations. 
> 
> Contact with other spacefaring civilizations was sparse: there were two other encounters, one of which was limited to generational ark–ships between the stars of a globular cluster for its empire, not knowing about the Dark Field or the technology of Catenas. The other was aware of Catenas, but was hostile to external interactions.
> 
> The Syranui Union splintered over economic and ideological differences, into various countries that reunited into the Syranui Federation. Under Federation control, there was a coup and establishment of a benevolent dictatorship with a unique idea: if Catenas can manipulate Reality, can Reality become as the individual wishes? The Syranui started constructing new Realities with Catenas: creating heaven-like conditions for people living in them. However, many were opposed to this as it became a toy for the rich and required external maintenence. Wars broke out, and the Catenic Heavens were mostly destroyed at great cost. Many of these constructs were based on Yruil and Helar, both being heavily destroyed.
> 
> Many individuals fled the chaos of what became known as the Heavenly Wars, out into the backwaters of Syranui space. Cetuāŋa was one of these backwater systems, and in it was a little–known planet called Iqenva. Splinter groups found these reclusive places, some stealing Catenas to create and destroy wormholes as they went, traversing system to system in search of a new home.
> 
> The Syranui that came to Iqenva found a neolithic people they called the Reithi. All of the Syranui present agreed on one, common tenet that none of them should ever break: protect Iqenva from the Heavenly Wars. Some Syranui present decided to live on the other planets and moons of Cetuāŋa, not Iqenva itself. Most decided to inhabit the planet, genetically engineering themselves to be compatible with the local biosphere, severing their ties to Yruil once and for all. Many of the Syranui, disillusioned with advanced technology, deliberately decided to live simpler lives, as herdsfolk and in small communities. These would become the progenitors for the Low Felari.
> 
> The High Felari were far rarer, only in the dozens and with merely 8 individuals that feature prominently in the affairs of Iqenva. They were the ones that, after all of the wars and conflicts over them, decided to continue with Catenic experiments. They were careful, but wanted to help the Reithi. The issues arose when they began to have disagreements over *how* they would help the Reithi. Some created and placed Catenas around the planet, aimed to help the lives and ease the technological ascendance of the Reithi. This would become [[Literamancy]], the effects of which on Reithi development the Syranui had no ideas of.
> 
> The Wars of Wings and the Nightborn Era would be the tipping point over the discontent many of the Syranui–turned–High Felari had with one another. Some, like Qirāsu and Asxiýr wanted to only act defensively against intrusions to the Cētu system, while others like Qiriýtaq wanted to unify the Reithi by force to elevate them faster against the threats of the Heavenly Wars. This conflict would spur internal Reithi violence and divisions, and the deaths of hundreds of thousands in campaigns. None of the High Felari dared to directly engage with one another, out of fear of destroying the planet. The outcome would be Qiriýtaq creating more of his own Catenas, sealing off the continent of [[Anselesna]] to pursue his goals, walking his own path to the planet. Other High Felaru saw the conflict and wanted to shield innocent people from it: the High Felaru Hyanéq made offers to groups on Anselesna of a new life in the oceans to escape the tyranny of Qiriýtaq, becoming the first Velsari.
> 
> After the Wars of Wings, most of the High Felari came to the consensus that such an event could never happen again: they maintain a watch on Qiriýtaq and Anselesna, and (mostly) try to keep out of Reithi affairs, though several are now revered as gods or demigods. Some encourage it, others shun it. Reithi now wonder about the mythical wars of the past, how true the effects of them were in the annals of history, and speculate about the Felari connection to the Catenas and the founding of their very religions.

> [!The 8 Gods|c-purple]-
> 
> - Qiriýtaq
> 	The Sovereign of [[Anselesna]], and the God of the North. The most active of the High Felari, and the most radically intervening. Started the Wars of Wings by trying to unite most of the Sunside against the other High Felari out of force. Withdrew to Anselesna after the loss of the 2nd War of Wings, and undertook the Sealing of Anselesna. Seen as a divine figure in Anselesna, and as an ancient foe of the Cresani nations. The nemesis of Asxiýr.
> - Asxiýr
> 	Commonly worshipped in Gixanu and Cresanu, and the God of the South - though somewhat to his chagrin. Formed and organized the Pact of Ŋašilu against Qiriýtaq, and served as the advisor / de facto shadow ruler of generations of leaders across Našilu during the Nightborn Era. Has since withdrawn to the Dark Field, but sometimes still affects outcomes in Gixanu and Cresanu to watch and guard against Qiriýtaq. His name is scorned in Anselesna, and is the nemesis of Qiriýtaq.
> - Athriýc
> 	One of the more reclusive Velsari; sometimes seen as the God of Invention. Formerly an engineer, has great expertise with the Catenas and how they operate. He spies extensively on the activities of the other High Felari and ambitious Reithi, has several guilds of spies in his name to keep the uneasy peace. Seen as the Patron of both Spies and Inventors.
> - Isaruiqé
> 	Primarily concerns herself with the affairs of Istarthu. While one of the less intervening gods, she has a few religions on the continent. Kind–hearted, has subtly affected the affairs of Istarthu to ensure compassionate and less ambitious rulers end up in systems of government. In a relationship with Renātac.
> - Tiýqla
> 	The God of the Dark. She has a similar role to Athriýc, but looks outwards to monitor the Heavenly Wars and the Felari in the rest of Cetuāŋa. Rarely worshipped on Iqenva, but has installed a few satellite systems around select Iqenva and other planets. Often assists the Starside of Iqenva, and liaisons with the Low Felari.
> - Renātac
> 	The God of Healing and Diplomacy. One of the most opposed to war and conflict, Renātac sponsors and helps people who show promise at furthering his goals and who are disadvantaged in life. Primarily helping Nulâašilu due to its fragile politics. In a relationship with Isaruiqé due to their shared tenets of healing and lesser interventionism.
> - Hyanéq
> 	The God of the Velsari. Contacted the folk of the Haqalu Archipelago during the Wars of Wings, offering them a new life under her tutelage in the seas. Her actions in creating a whole new species of sentient beings with a fanatical devotion to her drew mixed reactions from the other gods, but her fierce opposition to Qiriýtaq made her a necessary ally during the 2nd War of Wings. Generally independent of the other 8 gods.
> - Qirāsu
> 	The God of Industry. Sees the best way to protect Iqenva as elevating them technologically, often has to quarrel with the other gods on principles of non–interventionism to achieve his goals. Created Literamancy and oversaw the rise of Ancurāna, and then Haxīr. Plays a middle game between the active and inactive Felari gods.

> ## High Felari Life
> The different High Felari choose to live on Iqenva in different ways:
> - Some choose to live within the Dark Field, via Catenas stolen during their flight from the Heavenly Wars and brought with them for personal use. This is what the majority of the High Felari choose to do, placing their Catenas in safe places on the planet. Some of these realities overlap within the Dark Field due to proximity, and offer a form of connection between them, allowing for High Felari communities within the Dark Field overlaying Iqenva. As such, these High Felari do not interfere much with Reithi affairs, staying within the Dark Field instead—though they may visit and travel the planet in disguise. The 8 Gods often communicate here in a public forum hall, some more than others.
>   
> - The other option is for them to live on Iqenva itself. They will live with the Reithi, and generally form their own religions and kingdoms. This was most prevalent during the Wars of Wings, but still occurs to the modern day by a handful such as Qiriýtaq and Hyanéq among the Anselesnans and Ilyāni respectively. The High Felari who do this are often frowned upon for their interference with Iqenvan development. They may retain their own Dark Field safe houses as a contingency, but generally do not travel to the Iqenvan Dark Field.
>   
>   ## Limitations of High Felari Power
>   
>   The High Felari, while able to erase Iqenvan life, all share the common goal of protecting Iqenva and its life from the Heavenly Wars still ongoing beyond Cetuāŋa. Their internal conflict merely arises from how they wish to best protect the planet, and what "protecting the planet" strictly means.
>   
>   This forms a balance of power where the more active High Felari (primarily those living on Iqenva) have to sate the interests of those that wish for less interference (generally those in the Dark Field) and vice versa. The Reithi are often used as pawns in this way, where the active High Felari (such as Qiriýtaq and Hyanéq) spread propaganda and twist the truth about the intentions of the other High Felari to create a loyal base of support. The inactive High Felari have to break their own tenets to combat this, and to quietly support Reithi who agree with them and their goals.
