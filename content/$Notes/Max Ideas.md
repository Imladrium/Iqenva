- Catenas have a finite amount of effect upon the world, concentrated spherically around it
- People and other living things become "attuned" to living near a powerful (Literamantic, Aquamantic) catena, which makes them more powerful but also will result in withdrawal symptoms (severe cases, death) if not within the vicinity of a catena
- Dānura is made a condominium because of equal access to the catena for the Ancu Realms, and so that people may immigrate there (previously was restricted). Dānurans still trapped on the Island and the Outer Shores because of catenic withdrawal 
- Wealthy Ancu often move to Ráemar to attune more power when using glyphs, and to privatize catenic power
- Sastranivê is formed as a plantation for literic oils around the catena there; soon becomes a full–fledged colonial state, Dānurans move there en masse for proximity to the catena to not suffer symptoms while being more free than on the Island, independent state is established: a rival to the old order
- Schism between Sâiýme and Cāniše is due to the opinions of how much sway mortals on Dānura empowered by the Catena have over the doctrines of Sâî
- Haxīr wishes to expand eastwards into Mezāvca to aquire and develop literic farmlands, taking power away from the Ancu Realms (also for cultural reasons, Mezāvcans are kindred to the Haxīri)
- Emnity between Ancurāna and Gixanu oscillates with time; sometimes they work together to quell rebellions and wars against their frozen order; Haxīr, Esaceš, and Azušuŧ pose the greatest threats
- Glyphs may sometimes be "subcatenas" where they too may locally affect the interactions between the Dark Field and Reality, in unexpected ways; highglyphs allow for people to become subcatenas and gain powers (Halua of Isim). Esoteric, border–line occult, a true magic
- Anselesna's Felari god uses unique catenas to wall off the continent to non–followers, after the Nightborn Era
- Starside catena users use their own blood as a literic oil
