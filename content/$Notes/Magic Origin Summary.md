- A precursor species, the Syranui, evolve without magic on a planet called Yruil
	- The Syranui, over thousands of years, accrue and expand their technological knowledge to create gateway networks between the stars, emulating wormholes connecting systems, orbiting the extremities of each star in their polity
	- Rooted in atheism, the Syranui elect a government–turned–cult that makes it a priority to become gods themselves
	- The Syranui discover a way to forcibly pin a hitherto–unknown field / underlying substratum of the universe, the Dark Field, into reality with Catenas
- Catenas are ‘anchors’ or ‘pins’ that locally force the dark field to interact with the rest of reality, with each Catena capable of harnessing the Dark Field a different way, leaching it into reality
	- (Catenas are physically present in the Dark Field, with smaller sections in Reality)
	- Reality bleeds locally into the Dark Field too, creating mirror worlds of a few dozen hundred kilometres or so on the other side of each Catena (before Reality disintegrates slowly with increasing distance, as magic does in Reality)
- Catenas become the pinnacle of Syranui advancement, allowing for the creation of tailored systems where the Syranui can literally create their own, local laws overlapping those of Reality
	- The Syranui power over their physical empire withers as a result of their own creations though, with many engrossed in these perfect, heavenly realities they made for themselves as opposed to the physical universe. A smaller population takes care of the Catenas supporting the otherwise–impossible areas of mass entertainment and heaven for those in it
	- Some Syranui splinter, seeing Catenas as abhorrent, forming a new polity without them, though this comes at great cost in a war. 
	- Some Catenic Heavens crumble and wars are waged over ideologies regarding them, some are untouched.
	- Other splinter still, going their own ways, becoming gods of other planets or helping in their advancements
	- Syranui society decays away in the extremities of their former empire, leaving traces with former, weaker, experimental Catenas scattered about on random planets, eventually found by inhabitants
	- Some Catenas form horrible accidents befalling worlds, others are more perfected and form local ‘magic systems’ to a given world
- Iqenva is one such world with a series of ‘good’ Catenas. Magic in this particular case is taken up by many plants, which can ‘concentrate’ it into distillable liquids with which symbols may be drawn to effectuate commands. These symbols seem fundamental to the inhabitants of Iqenva, but are actually snippets of words to the Syranui. Combine these words in the right way with these written glyphs, and one achieves a particular effect on Reality.
	- This is Literamancy! The primary magic system central to Iqenva
	- There are some other Catenas, more unknown, on Iqenva that can effectuate non–Literamantic commands in their vicinities (other magic systems like Aquamancy and Isomancy)
	- Some of the gods worshipped in Iqenva are actually benevolent Syranui, who subtly aid in the development of the planet and may create afterlives for their followers
	- The continent of Anselesna is under the strong influence of one of the former Syranui, who developed his own catenas for his followers and walled the continent off from the rest of the world
	- Travel to the Dark Field is mysterious and unknown; deliberately sabotaged by the Syranui
- The Iqenvan system has hyperlanes made by the Syranui far out in the system, as well as an abandoned Catenic research base on a gas giant moon, opening the door to a space opera setting for Iqenva… :)
- Iqenvan life has reacted curiously to the presence of the more powerful catenas (Literamancy, Aquamancy, and Isomancy): 
	- Certain lifeforms have taken up the ability to refine the energy from catenas and use it as a food source, often microbial life that mutualistically inhabits a specific plant alike to how algae may inhabit a coral. These microbes often supplement the plant's growth, allowing them to grow far faster than other comparable plants.
	- The consumption of "catenic plants" or anything grown in the vicinity of a powerful catena can transfer these microbes, a rare few of which were able to adapt to a new ecosystem inside the Reithi. While these microbes are plentiful around catenas, they are nearly non–existent in their mutated forms elsewhere. 
	- People who inhabit the region close to catenas often become hosts, and these microbes symbiotically help provide energy to their hosts in exchange for protection. This means people living near powerful catenas generally are more powerful, but also will suffer withdrawal if they move too far abroad for too long a time as the microbes die inside of them. In severe cases, this can result in death.
	- This phenomenon, known in most of Nulâašilu as being "Islebound" after the inhabitants of Dānura who are often affected and bound to the island & surrounding area by it, has resulted in decrees prohibiting immigration and visitation of Dānura to most people in Ancu society, and the formation of a distinct Dānuran identity. Other catenas resulted in the migration of Dānurans to other places as a safe place for them to live with their condition.
