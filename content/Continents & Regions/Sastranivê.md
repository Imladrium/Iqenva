Sastranivê is a large archipelago in the tropics of Iqenva, collectively considered one of the continents. Sastranivê is characterized by volcanic highlands and estuarine, marshy lowlands which form one of the most unique environments of the planet due to its relative isolation. The climate varies much more with altitude than it does by distance from the Sunpole.

Sastranivê was discovered and settled well after the other continents of the planet. The archipelago is largely divided into the eponymous Sastrāni west and centre, the Nríyan east, and the Ilyāni seas & smaller islands.

# Natural History and Resources
The largest island of Sastrānivê, known in Ancu as Celdānya and in Pera Nrí as Kelda, was once a part of southern Istarthu before a rift opened between them. A brief connection with Tunčne during the supercontinent of Saryašna allowed for an intermingling of the ecological realms in the region, before the land was sundered from the larger continents. The subduction zone that spurred the rift's creation then started to build volcanic islands and mountain ranges along the spine of the forming island chain, which along with a small igneous province formed the bulk of the highlands.

The sediment washed down into the continental shelf on the northern side, creating vast lowlands which quickly become host to thriving jungle ecosystems. The plants of Sastranivê are almost entirely dominated by bright, vibrant red hues in order to reflect much of the ever-present rays of Cētu beaming down upon them. The lowlands are some of the warmest places on the planet; the Reithi largely inhabit the coasts and highlands.
