Nulâašilu is a region / subcontinent located on the continent of Našilu, in the southwestern [[Sunside]] and [[Starside#Duskring|Duskring]]. The region is dominated by three landforms—the outer volcanic island arc (the [[Alšudês]]) southern lowlands (Nulâašilu Proper) and the northern highlands (the [[Halevnê|Halevnic Plateau]]), resulting in a wide form of terrain types, natural resources, and a general isolation from the rest of the continent. Its location near to the 90° meridian results in a wide variety of climates, with an eastern subtropical coast featuring both arid and humid regions, and a western tundra abutting an iceberg–filled ocean.

Nulâašilu is divided into several religions, cultures, and zones of influence, most of which may be divided between the [[Ancu]], [[Iŋiŷn|Iŋiŷni]], [[Halevnê|Halevnu]], and [[Gixanu|Gixanê]] cultural spheres.


# Natural History and Resources
Nulâašilu was formerly an independent continent, over 400 MYA⊕, being the only landmass to have stayed continuously on the Sunside since the migration of life onto land before that time; thus it was at one point the only bastion of terrestrial life on Iqenva. In the following eons, however, Nulâašilu migrated west and collided with Ŋašilu Proper, in the process forming the Halevnic Plateau and the [[Tamaniš Basin]] to its north. It was subsequently part of Saryašna, the most recent supercontinent 300–200 MYA⊕, which due to its position over the [[Sunside|Sunpole]] caused a mass extinction. It remained an integral part of Ŋašilu during the breakup of Saryašna, although still distinguished from it due to the Halevnic Plateau, severely weathered but still a formidable obstacle. This helps it maintain a slightly distinctive ecological makeup compared to the rest of Ŋašilu, particularly the islands of the Alšudes.

The subcontinent is middling in terms of resources, with the greatest concentration of metallic deposits in the forelands south of the Halevnic Plateau. Other reserves can be found in the highlands of Mablus in [[Haxīr]], Halevnê Proper, and the Hinuvêtan Mountains of [[Êîlur]]. Expansive peat reserves and marshes can be found in the lowlands of [[Esaceš]] and [[Nariŷn]], while coal deposits are most plentiful in northeastern [[Iŋatra]]. 

# Political History

Migrations to Nulâašilu began back over 10 000 years⊕ ago in the late neolithic age, largely along the eastern shore of Ŋašilu from the [[Cresanu|north]]. The natural geography insulating Nulâašilu, namely the Halevnic Plateau and the narrow passes to either side, discouraged earlier migrations. The western passes through modern Esaceš are frigid tundra, partially in darkness, while the eastern coast in modern [[Gixanu]] is an arid desert.
