# Athaqar'Raxu (AR)

AR comprises of all events before the Nightborn Era. The Reithi advance from hunter–gatherer to complex societies, forming the first true polities and empires around the advent of the Sunborn era, often seen as a golden age before the wars that follow. 

Meanwhile the Felari start scheming against one another, with interpersonal politics triumphing over the welfare of the planet they swore to defend.

# Athaqar'Vamu (AV)

AV is synonymous with the Nightborn Era—an era of intercontinental conflict and warfare, spurred on by the High Felari as they scheme on how to best advance Iqenvan interests. Instead, they drag the planet back, and cause the collapse of empires. Nevertheless, technology develops, encouraged by the sparsity of the times. AV is often considered the Age of the Felari.

Ŋašilu in particular is affected, with populations migrating to and fro to avoid the front lines of war. The era ends with the Sealing of Anselesna, and the Détente.

# Athaqar'Esu (AE)

If AV is known as the Age of the Felari, then AE is known as the Age of the Reithi. The High Felari mostly withdraw from planetary affairs, going into hiding. The Reithi reassert control over their affairs once more, and technology long kept restricted from the wars flourishes. Expeditions start intercontinental trade and conquests, and more recently industrial ventures capitalize upon a deeper understanding of the nature of the world, and of the Catenas.

New ideologies take root, and revolutions start to take over the many autocracies and literacracies of the world. Old empires and powers fall, new ones assert their places. Several Catenas exchange hands as cities fall and rise. The Velsari come out of their long hiding, and begin to take a more active role in politics.
